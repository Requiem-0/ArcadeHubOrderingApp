Key journeys

The paths a customer actually takes, in order. Red steps are where the journey stops working.

Order food at the table Stops at checkout

Home→ Drawer · Food & Drinks Menu→ Product detail · size, add-ons→ Add to cart→ Cart→ Sign-in sheet if guest→ Checkout · route missing→ Order success

There is no direct way into the menu from the Home screen itself; customers reach it through the drawer, a zone page, or the empty-cart button. Checkout is built but the router has no path for it, so the button fails.

Book a gaming session Nothing reaches the venue

Home→ Zone card or bento tile→ Zone detail · Book→ Service booking · accept terms→ Confirm · waits 1 s, sends nothing→ Order success · fake order

The customer ends on the order success screen, which shows a hardcoded order for nachos and mocktails rather than their booking.

Find the venue Works

Home · scroll to Find us→ Tap building or pin→ 4th floor plan sheet

Or “Get directions” on the same card opens Google Maps with the venue as the destination.

Check a past order Works when signed in

Orders tab→ Order card→ Order detail · Placed, Kitchen, Served

Guests see a sign-in prompt on the Orders tab instead of a list.

Launch and sign-in

No bottom tabs on these screens. The app never forces sign-in: everyone lands on Home, and sign-in is asked for only at checkout, booking, or from the drawer and Profile.
